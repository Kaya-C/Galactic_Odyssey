Years pass since your triumphant return from the war back to your kingdom. Peace has been restored to the Ithena and the Greek Galaxy.

With your strategic brilliance mind and new resilience, ascend to the throne once again.

Under your wise rule, the planet flourishes as it becomes a major hub for interstellar trade and exploration and technological advancements. 

You are reunited with your wife and son and spend your rest of your life as a united family.

Time, once said to not go in a straight line by Tiresias ultimately did for you as you slowly aged.
