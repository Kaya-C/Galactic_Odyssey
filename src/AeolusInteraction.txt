After 10 days through several hyperspaces, you are still failing to get closer to the Greek Galaxy

It seems as though cosmic currents are pushing you back and delaying your trip home

There is only one being who is capable of controlling such currents, Poseidon, a cosmic deity

As a cosmic cyclone starts to take form, your ship, is caught in the gravitational field of a ringed planet and pulled in

It appears you've entered Aeolia, the home planet of Aeolus, a celestial being who commands the interstellar winds.

In the quest to ask help from Aeolus, you travel to  the heart of the planet, seeking his celestial powers

But its seem as though Aeolus instead has sought you out, as your ship begins to move in mesmerizing circles
