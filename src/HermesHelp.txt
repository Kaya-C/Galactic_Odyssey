Just as you're about to become one with the matter within the black hole, Hermes, the messenger of the celestial being, appears in a gleaming manifestation. 

Hermes said that Athena, inspired by your wisdom throughout your journey, has appealed to Zeus about your release from the Black Hole and a way home.

Hermes was the one called for this divine intervention, with the speed of a cosmic messenger, Hermes breaks you  from the gravitational clutches of Calypso and passes the event horizon. 

As reality shifts once again, you find yourself back in space. 

Though only a few hours have passed for you, outside, seven years have unfolded. 

Hermes drops you on the Planet of Ogygia and gives you some supplies to build a new spaceship.
