As you journey away from Thrinakia, the light outside the ship intensifies as interstellar dust merges into a reflection nebula pulsating with energy bursts.
            
The gravity of the gas clouds triggers nuclear fusion, and before your eyes, you witness the birth of a star in a mere fraction of the usual time; something or someone of a higher power is involved.
            
Suddenly, the remaining gases envelop the spaceship along with strikes of radiant energy, a signature of Zeus' formidable power.
            
As the ship's systems begin to break down, panic arises among the crew. Alarms start blaring, and the ship's lights go out

You attempt to calm the crew, but sudden beams of light hits the ship.
            
Amid the system's catastrophic failure, the ship is torn apart by the relentless force of beams. Some instantly die from being exposed to the cold vacuum of space while others are hit by the light and disintegrate before your eyes.
