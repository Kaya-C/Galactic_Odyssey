As your spaceship journey through the vastness of space, you come upon the lushful and bountiful planet of Sicily

Hoping to get some source of energy for the ships, you descent with 12 of your crewmates in shuttles

As you roam the planet, your crew stumbles upon a cave filled with robot sheeps, a source of power enough to charge your fleet of 12 spaceships

.........

Suddenly, a low, ominous unnatural sound echos through the cave.

Polyphemus, a one-eyed colossal robot with a metal club, appears and rolls a huge stone in front of the cave entrance, trapping your crew inside.
Angered by unwanted the intruders, he begins to stomp on your crewmates.

The situation is dire. Avoid his club and try to attack when given the chance .
