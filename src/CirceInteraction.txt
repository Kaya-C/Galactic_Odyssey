Running low on supplies and drained of energy, you listen to Perimedes' advice to go to a nearby planet named Aeaea.
As the Homer 1200 enters the planet's atmosphere, a breathtaking sight unfolds—a landscape adorned with lush grasslands and dense woodlands, a place similar to home.
        
Your second in command, Eurylochus offers to explore the planet along with 22 other individuals.

                                      

After a few hours with no communication, you grow worried

Venturing out of the ship, you come upon a tired Eurylochus who looks as though he was running away from something
He claims that a mad scientist turned all of the crewmates into pigs and tells you to leave the planets with the remaining crew and him.


As Captain, it is your decision.
